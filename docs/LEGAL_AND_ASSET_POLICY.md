# Legal, Naming, and Asset Policy

This project is an independent community effort. It must not imply that it is official, licensed, endorsed, or affiliated with the rights holders of an existing game franchise.

## Working title and franchise references
“IGI: REBORN” is a provisional internal working title only. Project name, logos, branding, and promotional language require IP review before public use. References to an existing franchise do not grant permission to use protected names, characters, story, maps, dialogue, visual identity, or other expressive material.

If permission is unavailable, transition to a distinct original title, setting, characters, story, missions, and visual identity before public promotion or distribution.

## Asset requirements
Every committed asset must be one of:
- Created by a contributor who grants the project documented rights for intended use.
- Commissioned under a written agreement covering project use and distribution.
- Public-domain with credible provenance.
- Licensed under terms compatible with intended distribution.

For third-party assets, record creator, source URL, licence name/version, attribution, modifications, and date obtained in the asset manifest.

## Prohibited contributions
Do not submit extracted or copied game files, models, textures, maps, sounds, music, animations, or source code from commercial games without permission. Do not submit ripped screenshots, logos, promotional art, or franchise character likenesses. Do not submit assets with unclear provenance or incompatible licences.

## Contributor declaration
By submitting content, contributors confirm they created it or have the rights to contribute it, and provide required attribution and licence information. Maintainers may reject or remove content with uncertain provenance.

## Release gate
Before public branding or release, obtain qualified legal review of the project name, marketing, code licence, contributor terms, and all asset licences. Community or noncommercial intent is not a substitute for permission or legal advice.
